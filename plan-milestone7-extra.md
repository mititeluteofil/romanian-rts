# Milestone 7 Extra: Additional Brainstorming Ideas from the Stand-Up Audio

This companion note collects additional satirical RTS ideas, units, events, and flavor mechanics drawn directly from the stand-up bit, complementing [plan-milestone7.md](plan-milestone7.md).

---

## 1. Stegarul („Auraș”) — Extended Mechanics
- **„Ne face și vânt” (Active Fanning Aura)**: Toggle or short-cooldown ability where Stegarul waves the banner vigorously, giving nearby friendly units a brief speed/attack-rate boost (useful in summer heat or long marches).
- **„Palma la fund” Rally Boost**: Newly trained units leaving `Tabără` while Stegarul is stationed nearby spawn with a short movement burst straight toward the rally point (*„cu fața de supărat”*).
- **„Cap de bour” & Principality Standards**: Unique animated banner graphics per principality (Moldova *Cap de bour*, Ardeal crest, Țara Românească eagle, Dobrogea trident/anchor).
- **„Bibelou” Unarmed Brawl**: Optional comical modifier/mode where medieval soldiers drop weapons in close quarters (*„cu pumnii ne batem!”*) and Stegarul still leads unarmed with `0` damage.

---

## 2. Scouting, Terrain & Fatigue
- **„Iscoade” (Scouts)**:
  - Cheap, fast `Iscoadă` unit trained at `Curte` / `Tabără` (`era: 0`) with wide sight radius to uncover fog-of-war, spot enemy buildings, and warn before Ottoman raids arrive.
  - Speaks disguised/garbled lines when tapped near an enemy court.
- **„Ne-am rătăcit / Îmi trag sufletul” (March Fatigue & Forward Camps)**:
  - On the bigger map, squads marching very long distances without stopping build up mild fatigue (`-15%` speed after crossing half the country, shouting *„Cred că ne-am rătăcit!”* / *„Stai să-mi trag sufletul!”*).
  - Resting briefly near a friendly `Han` or `Tabără` restores full speed immediately—encouraging forward bases along mountain passes.
- **„Teren mlăștinos la Rovine” (Dynamic Mud / Weather)**:
  - Periodic rain/mud patches near riverbanks and marshes (*„teren destul de mlăștinos, dar mai bun decât pe Ghencea”*) that temporarily slow units crossing plains unless roads/bridges are built.
- **„Am uitat ochelarii” (Fog-of-War / Night Sight)**:
  - If Fog-of-War is added later, Era 0 peasants start with modest vision radius (*„Moară mama dacă văd ceva”*) improved by building `Turn de veghe` (Watchtowers) or researching *Ochelari*.

## 3. Satirical Units, Sub-Factions & Events
- **Oltenia Mercenaries at Craiova (`PLACES.craiova`)**:
  - Neutral/capturable regional camp near Craiova: holding the area lets you ally with or recruit fiery *Olteni* reinforcements (*„te aliezi cu oltenii din Craiova”*).
- **Intimidation Aura (*„Doar prin puterea intimidării”*)**:
  - Upgrade *Băieți de băieți* (or *Interlopi* in Era 4) so even without attacking, standing near enemies lowers their damage by `20–25%` (*„Bagă sabia-n teacă că te tai singur!”*).
- **„Lăutari / Concert după victorie”**:
  - Support unit or `Han` upgrade that boosts nearby friendly morale and worker recovery after winning a skirmish (*„Concert Voltaj mâine, după victorie!”*).
- **Rare Battle Event — „Admiratorul fanatic (Eminescu)”**:
  - Rare comedic event during large clashes: a lone *poet* sprints across the battlefield, briefly distracting nearby units on both sides (`stun = 20`) before vanishing into the woods.
- **„Live Match Commentator” Toast Mode**:
  - Optional HUD toggle (*„Comentator sportiv”*) that announces big battles and raids like a Romanian football broadcast (*„FC Imperiul Otoman vs Sport Club Țara Românească — strategie defensivă 2-2-2447!”*).

## 4. Idle Peasant Satire („Italia” & Tavern Wandering)
- **„Plecat în Italia”**:
  - Currently a flavor warning for long-idle Moldovan peasants; in an optional *Hardcore Satire* mode, peasants left idle for `>90s` in Era 3–4 actually pack up and *„pleacă în Italia”* (vanishing and freeing population cap, with a humorous toast).
- **„Umblă random pe hartă”**:
  - Peasants left idle next to a `Cazan de pălincă` for too long occasionally take a sip on their own and wander a couple of tiles randomly until given an order.

## 5. Modes & Presentation Touches
- **„v2.74 Beta” Endless Sandbox**:
  - Optional subtitle/mode inspired by *„România v2.74 beta — la noi nu ies lucrurile din prima și nu se termină niciodată”*, where matches continue endlessly with escalating Ottoman/ANAF/weather events even after reaching Prezent.
- **„Dobrogea Practice” Tutorial Scenario**:
  - Short guided onboarding mission starring Dobrogea (*„doar la modul Practice”*) teaching gathering, building, rally points, and abilities safely before jumping into 4-way FFA.
- **Pre-Battle Standoff Banter**:
  - When enemy armies face each other just outside aggro range across a river or pass, soldiers trade humorous stand-up banter (*„Stăm de patru ore, mă!”*, *„Ne-am înțeles la 2 fără 14 minute!”*) before the clash begins.
